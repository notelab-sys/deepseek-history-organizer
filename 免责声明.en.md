1. Marks such as ✅/⚠️/❌ generated by the Tool come from automated processes (e.g., Crossref/DOI resolution). They are "tendency" suggestions and **do not constitute conclusions about truthfulness**.
2. The marks only cover databases the Tool can access; Chinese literature may need additional checking on platforms such as CNKI, Wanfang, or VIP.
3. Before citing a reference, please verify each item — authors, year, title, journal, volume/issue/pages, and DOI — against authoritative databases such as PubMed, Web of Science, Crossref, and publisher websites.
